# About Holvek

Holvek is an all-in-one search engine optimization (SEO) plugin for WordPress. It helps website owners, content creators, and businesses make their content easy to find on search engines like Google and Bing, as well as modern AI-powered search tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.

---

## Why We Built Holvek

Most traditional SEO plugins have become bloated, complicated, and confusing. They overwhelm site owners with dozens of confusing settings, cluttered admin menus, and arbitrary color-coded scores that encourage keyword stuffing rather than good writing.

Search has also changed. Today, people discover websites not only through blue links on Google, but through AI overviews, answer engines, and mobile assistants.

Holvek was built to solve these problems with three simple ideas:

1. **Keep it lightweight:** An SEO plugin should never slow down your website. We keep our code clean, run heavy tasks in the background, and add minimal overhead to your pages.
2. **Make SEO easy to understand:** We explain recommendations in plain English without confusing acronyms or buzzwords.
3. **Be ready for both search engines and AI:** We help you structure your content so that both search engine bots and AI tools can read, understand, and cite your work accurately.

## How It Works

### Smart Defaults Right Out of the Box
The moment you install Holvek, it gets to work. The plugin automatically creates XML sitemaps, sets up standard Open Graph tags for social sharing, generates breadcrumbs, and adds valid Schema.org structured data. You do not need to configure dozens of complicated settings just to get started.

### Helpful On-Page Writing Guidance
When writing in the WordPress block editor (Gutenberg), Holvek gives you practical, actionable advice:
- Checks whether your title and description fit comfortably on mobile and desktop screens.
- Evaluates text readability using the established Flesch-Kincaid scale.
- Shows live previews of how your post will look on Google, Facebook, and Twitter/X.
- Suggests links to other relevant articles on your site to keep readers engaged.

### Search Engine Notifications
Holvek can use IndexNow to notify participating search engines about published URLs when an administrator enables it. Google’s Indexing API is restricted to eligible JobPosting and livestream pages; neither notification guarantees indexing.

### Built-in Technical Tools
You don't need five different plugins to manage basic website health. Holvek includes:
- A redirect manager for 301 and 302 redirects.
- A 404 error monitor to catch and fix broken URLs.
- A visual robots.txt editor with syntax validation.
- An optional Apache .htaccess editor, off until an administrator turns it on, with private backups.
- A broken link checker that finds dead hyperlinks in your content.

### Clear Performance & Search Insights
Connect Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 to see real traffic numbers, search clicks, and top-ranking keywords directly inside your WordPress dashboard.

### Optional AI Assistance
If you want help writing titles, meta descriptions, or summaries, you can connect your preferred AI provider (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Ollama) or use the Holvek remote service. Ollama defaults to this server. A remote Ollama host receives the text you submit. AI features are completely optional—the plugin works fully without any third-party AI keys.

## Our Core Guarantees

- **No Bloat:** We don't display intrusive banner ads, upsell popups, or unnecessary widgets in your WordPress admin.
- **Zero Lock-In:** Your data belongs to you. You can easily import data from Yoast SEO, Rank Math, or All in One SEO, or export your settings at any time.
- **Privacy-First:** We respect visitor privacy. Holvek includes a lightweight cookie notice that records a visitor’s choice; it does not replace a full consent-management solution for third-party scripts.
- **Open Standards:** Everything we generate adheres to official web standards, including Schema.org JSON-LD specifications, Google Search documentation, and emerging AI standards like `llms.txt`.
