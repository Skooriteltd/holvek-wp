# Holvek Product Roadmap

This document outlines planned and upcoming features for Holvek that are not yet implemented in the current plugin release.

---

## 1. AI Search & Citation Tracking

- **AI Citation Monitor:** Track how often your articles and brand are cited in answers generated by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Claude.
- **AI Visibility Trends:** View historical trends showing whether your site is gaining or losing visibility in AI search over 30, 60, and 90 days.
- **Citation Drop Alerts:** Receive email and dashboard notifications when AI platforms stop citing your key pages.

---

## 2. Autonomous Internal Linking Agent

- **Background Link Analysis:** A cloud-based helper that reads your full content library and suggests natural internal links with diverse anchor text.
- **Editorial Approval Queue:** Review and approve suggested links with one click inside the post editor before they are added to published articles.
- **Optional Auto-Insert:** An automated mode for high-volume publishers to automatically link newly published articles to existing relevant topics.

---

## 3. Team Collaboration & Editorial Workflows

- **Inline SEO Comments:** Leave notes and recommendations on specific headings, titles, or paragraphs for other writers and editors to review.
- **Publish Gating:** Optionally prevent articles from being published until all required SEO checklist items and editorial comments are resolved.
- **SEO Sign-Off Column:** Track which articles have received final SEO approval directly in the WordPress posts list.

---

## 4. Multi-Brand Agency Workspaces

- **Brand Profiles:** Switch between different brand profiles with separate SEO rules, social configurations, and tracking keys.
- **Centralized Agency Dashboard:** Manage multiple client websites and settings from a single unified view.

---

## 5. Cloud-Based Broken Link Crawler

- **High-Volume Scanning:** Scan enterprise websites with tens of thousands of pages using an off-site cloud crawler that does not consume server memory or slow down WordPress.
- **Scheduled Crawls:** Automatic weekly or monthly crawl reports with exportable CSV spreadsheets.

---

## 6. Client Reporting & White-Label Portal

- **Client-Facing Summary:** A clean, simplified web portal for clients to see their site's SEO health, ranking progress, and traffic trends.
- **Automated PDF Reports:** Generate branded, monthly PDF summaries for clients and stakeholders with one click.
- **Custom Branding:** Replace plugin branding with your agency's logo and color scheme.

---

## 7. Competitor Schema Scraper

- **Schema Importer:** Paste a competitor URL to analyze and import their structured data schema as a starting template for your own page.
- **JavaScript Rendering Support:** Cloud microservice to read schema from modern single-page applications and complex JavaScript sites.

---

## 8. Enterprise & Self-Hosted AI Gateways

- **Custom Private Endpoints:** Connect to corporate AI deployments (such as Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, or private VPC servers) with custom headers and strict data privacy.
- **Audit Logging for AI Prompts:** Maintain an enterprise compliance log of every prompt sent and received by editorial staff.

---

## 9. Framework-Agnostic PHP Core Package

- **Standalone Composer Package:** Separate the core SEO calculation engine (meta tag rules, sitemap generation, schema models) into a standalone PHP package for use in Laravel, Symfony, or headless Node.js backends.
